## Purpose

Translate what an author writes into meaning that already exists upstream. One relationship: an authored activation directive carries an evidence proposition.

## Owns

- The authored directive spelling, as ecosystem surface rather than semantic identity.
- The activation relationship: a directive paired with core's `EvidenceProposition`.
- Refusal of directives that cannot be translated, and of propositions that cannot be satisfied.

## Does not own

- A proposition grammar. Core owns the one; a target that authors its own has stopped translating and started competing.
- Execution backend selection. The compiler and backend planner choose from requirements and evidence.
- Island identity, graph-cut joining, evidence-source declaration, egress selection, operation mounting, or target configuration. Each has an owner and none is here.

## Collapsing nine directives without building a kingdom

One authoring directive must not absorb settlement, remote evidence, backend selection, and effect meaning. Those relationships may share a pleasant authoring projection while remaining separate contracts underneath.

The risk in collapsing them is obvious: one central directive becomes one drawer holding all nine meanings. That is what the absence laws are for. The relationships can share one pleasant authoring experience while remaining separate contracts underneath — an author directive is not a backend dropdown wearing HTML.

## Laws

- The activation proposition is core's, compared against the imported authority — `Equal` is invariant, so a structurally identical local twin fails.
- An authored directive selects no execution backend: `backend`, `worker`, `gpu`, `wasm`, `execution`, and `runtime` are absent by law.
- The activation directive holds no foreign meaning: eight further keys are absent, so nine spellings cannot reassemble under one name.
- A refusal names what it refused and why.
- Translation and refusal stay distinct outcomes.

## Proof obligations

- That every recognised directive spelling maps to exactly one proposition.
- That an unrecognised directive never silently produces an always-true activation.

Assurance-and-implementation territory.

## Implementation boundary

A realization must satisfy the laws and proof obligations above through this home's declared authorities.
